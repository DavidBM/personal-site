Declared and connected

Contracts connect registered services. Source links open the owning file. External stages describe explicit dependencies; they do not imply live connectivity.

Observed contract totals

Broker aggregates across all instances and scopes. Service and trace filters cannot attribute these totals to individual edges. Queue age and handler time are unavailable here.

Direct streams

Bulk buffers never pass through the inspector. Progress samples come from separate worker queries; they are not simultaneous. Applied means CPU/GPU buffer publication, not GPU completion.

Command timeline

Each clock has its own axis. Local durations are measured within one process; cross-clock positions are not synchronized. Parent span IDs preserve causality. At most 256 rows per clock are drawn.
