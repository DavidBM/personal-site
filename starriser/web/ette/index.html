Your civilization
The galaxy

One world. All of it yours.

Your civilization was born on a single planet in the Federation Core. Every coast, mine and city on it belongs to you, and the planet itself is your interface: you work on the real globe, not on a flat menu.

Your first year is spent making it the envy of the Core.

FIELD NOTESOwned outright, freeResearch happens on planetsPlanets never run dry

Heroes are moving cities.

Growth does not come from more planets. It comes from heroes: a flagship, a city of factory and mining ships around it, and the fleet that flies with it.

Point a hero at the dark and it brings civilization along.

FIELD NOTESOutposts & asteroid mining: week twoFactory ships: around month threeCrewed by your home world

Every ton has to be flown.

Credits, licences and orders travel instantly over the galactic network. Everything else does not. Ships are the only link between worlds, so every trade ends with a hold, a route and a pilot.

FIELD NOTESSix combat classes, interceptor to colossusBase travel is freePropellant is a booster

Everything is for sale.

Ships, cargo, maps, licences: everything can be bought and sold. Federation markets charge a visible 0.5% fee, and that is all. What other games call an exploit, this galaxy calls business.

No pay-to-win. Real money buys tradable premium items, which players sell for credits. If you would rather grind, the same benefits are within reach.

Piracy is a train robbery.

Raiders clamp onto a moving convoy, fight off the guards, force a hold, load what they can and run. The convoy keeps going and arrives short.

Escorts, routes and timing are the answer. So is becoming the raider.

FIELD NOTESBoarding, not just destructionWrecks can be strippedHeroes keep a protected hold

The world answers. The interface doesn't.

Nothing is greyed out. Break Federation law and the Federation sends a fleet, in person. Large enough alliances have been known to send it back.

And whatever you do out there, the Core will still take you home.

FIELD NOTESNo blocked actionsConsequences arrive by shipThe Federation can be beaten

The Core is only the beginning.

Around the end of your first year, control-centre ships carry your flag outward. They land, become buildings and claim regions of other worlds, in a universe of tens of thousands of star systems.

FIELD NOTESOne persistent universeRegions from about year oneMaps are earned or bought

Shields, not vacation mode.

The universe keeps going when you log off. Raise an 8-hour or 24-hour shield while you sleep, work or live your life, and come back to an empire that is still yours.

FIELD NOTES8 h and 24 h shieldsPlan around them. So will they.
The desk

Your empire runs in a tab.

Remember checking your fleet between classes? It's back, and it finally looks the way you imagined it: a full 3D galaxy, on desktop and phone, with nothing to install.

  • Same universe, same rivals, years at a time.
  • Quick orders between meetings; deep planning when you have the evening.
  • Built by people who set alarms for fleet saves.
The market

Everything is for sale. Everything has to be flown.

One market across the Core, priced by players. Buy anywhere, then move it: the credits travel instantly, the cargo waits in a hold until a ship carries it home.
