The galaxy is a market.

Everything can be bought and sold: ore, hulls, crews' contracts, a whole flagship. Federation markets charge a visible 0.5% fee, and that's all. What other games call an exploit, this galaxy calls business.

A purchase is a promise, not a teleport. The credits move instantly over the galactic network; the cargo waits in a hold until a ship carries it home.

05 · Law

The world answers, not the interface.

Nothing is greyed out. Break Federation law and the Federation sends a fleet, a real one, flying in from a real station. Large enough alliances can beat it. Whether that is wise is a separate question.

Piracy

Piracy is a train robbery.

Raiders clamp onto a moving convoy, fight off the guards, force a hold, load what they can and run. The convoy keeps going and arrives short. Escorts, routes and timing are the counters.

Credits · the currency that matters

No pay-to-win. Money is a market too.

Real money buys tradable premium items, and players sell them to other players for credits. Players who prefer to grind can reach the same benefits. Whatever you hold, someone earned it or bought it from someone who did.
