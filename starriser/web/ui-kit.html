Internal · design system

UI kit

Every component here is drawn by assets/starchad.css, the same stylesheet the pages use. The states shown side by side are the real rules, forced on with .is-hover, .is-active and .is-focus.

Amber means you can act. One amber button per view. It is never used for decoration.
Blue informs; it never reacts. Data, live status and links inside data are blue. Hover, focus and selection never turn anything blue.
Focus is a bone ring. The same ring on every control, inside the chamfer on buttons.
Working keeps its colour. A busy button shows a sweep and ignores the pointer. Nothing in the interface is disabled; the world answers instead.
Gold belongs to Founders. The gold seal, crest and tags appear only around the beta and Founder rewards.
Hover never changes a choice. Selected chips, tabs and toggles look the same under the pointer.

Buttons

Three variants and three sizes. The chamfer comes from the ships' faceted hulls. The "Working" column is what a button shows after you press it while the request is on its way.

Icons

One sprite (src/partials/icons.html), drawn on a 24 px grid with a 1.5 px line and mitred corners. Filled marks appear only where the brand chevron does: hero, fleet, browser. Use with <svg class="icon"><use href="#i-planet"/></svg>.

Seals

Medallion images from the Codex image tool, with the lettering set as live text on the blank band, so it stays sharp and can be translated. Silver is the seal of the game; gold is the Founders' seal.

Wordmarks and marks

The Λ letters double as rank chevrons and ship prows. The amber marks the chad in StarChad and StarChadette, and the word GIGA in the beta marks. Logos are sized by height so the editions match.
